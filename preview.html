About Us

The NMSU Triathlon Club is dedicated to fostering a vibrant community for triathlon enthusiasts at New Mexico State University. We provide training, support, and opportunities to participate in triathlons while promoting health and fitness. We compete across the country in various triathlon distances ranging from full Ironman distance to sprint and relay triathlons.

Past Competitions

Collegiate Club Nationals — Gulfport, MS

March 2026

The team rented a van and drove 17 hours overnight to the collegiate nationals race in Gulfport, Mississippi. This was another great experience for the team, with an ocean swim, low elevation, a flat bike, and flat run. Similar to Miami, the Olympic race has almost 1,000 entrants split among multiple waves. NMSU Triathlon Club had five representatives at the race: four athletes and one team photographer.
